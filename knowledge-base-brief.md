# Knowledge Base Brief

The organisation the helpdesk supports, and the policies its knowledge base describes (task 5.3). Task 5.4 writes the knowledge base articles from this brief, and task 5.5 loads them into the AI's prompt.

Adapted from a support knowledge base supplied by Ahmed, for a fictional organisation, with its rules brought into line with `project-scope.md` and `implementation-plan.md`.

## The organisation

**Mahjoub Academy** is a fictional school that sells online programming courses. Each course is bought once and kept for life. Students watch video lessons in their browser, download the source code, and receive a certificate when they complete a course.

Students write to **support@helpdesk.mahjoub.io**, the address this helpdesk receives on. Every answer they get comes back from that address, in the same email thread, so "reply to this email" is always how they reach us again.

## How the helpdesk handles a request

Every email becomes a ticket, and the AI puts it in one of three categories:

| Category | Covers | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| General | Accounts, logins, purchases, lifetime access, certificates, coupons, account changes | The AI answers from this knowledge base and the ticket is set Resolved |
| Technical | Video playback, video quality, downloads | The AI answers from this knowledge base and the ticket is set Resolved |
| Refund | Any request for money back, including a disputed charge or a chargeback | The AI drafts a reply, and a support agent reviews and approves it before it is sent |

A ticket is also handed to an agent when the AI cannot produce a reply after retrying.

Two situations have no route of their own in the implementation plan, and the AI answers them like any other message: a student threatening legal action, and a concern about the security of their account.

## 1. Account and login

### I forgot my password

1. Go to the login page.
2. Click **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter the email address you registered with.
4. Follow the instructions in the reset email.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam or promotions folder.

### I'm not receiving the password reset email

Possible reasons:

- The email address was mistyped.
- The account was created with a different email address.
- The email went to your spam folder.

If it has not arrived within 10 minutes, reply to this email and we will look into it.

## 2. Course access and purchases

### I bought a course but cannot see it

Possible reasons:

- You are signed in with a different email address from the one you bought with.
- The payment is still processing.
- The purchase did not complete.

Your receipt email confirms whether the purchase went through.

### Can I transfer a course to another account?

No. A course belongs to the account that bought it and cannot be transferred.

## 3. Lifetime access

### What does lifetime access mean?

- You pay once.
- You keep access for good.
- You receive every future update to that course.

Lifetime access covers only the course you bought.

## 4. Refunds

### What is the refund policy?

- Refunds can be requested within 30 days of purchase.
- No refund is given once 80% or more of the course has been completed.
- Every refund request is reviewed by a member of our support team, who confirms whether it qualifies and how much is refunded, in full or in part.
- An approved refund is processed within 5 to 10 business days.

### How do I request a refund?

Reply within 30 days of purchase with:

1. Your order receipt.
2. The reason for your request.

A member of our support team will review it and reply to confirm.

## 5. Certificates

### Do you provide certificates?

Yes. A certificate is issued when you complete a course, and you can find it in your dashboard. Certificates are not accredited degrees.

## 6. Downloading content

### Can I download the videos?

- Videos are streamed online and cannot be downloaded for offline viewing.
- Source code can be downloaded.

## 7. Technical problems

### Videos are not playing

Try the following:

- Clear your browser cache.
- Use the latest version of Chrome or Edge.
- Turn off browser extensions.
- Check your internet connection.

### The video quality is low

Video quality adjusts automatically to your internet speed. A stable connection gives the best quality.

## 8. Coupon codes

### My coupon code doesn't work

Possible reasons:

- The coupon has expired.
- The coupon has already been used.
- The coupon does not apply to the course you chose.

Only one coupon can be used per purchase.

## 9. Account changes

### How do I change my email address?

Reply with:

- Your current email address.
- Your new email address.
- Proof of purchase, if we ask for it.

Our team makes the change once your request is confirmed.
