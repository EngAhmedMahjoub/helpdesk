# Account and login

### I forgot my password

1. Go to the login page.
2. Click **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter the email address you registered with.
4. Follow the instructions in the reset email.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam or promotions folder.

### I'm not receiving the password reset email

Possible reasons:

- The email address was mistyped.
- The account was created with a different email address.
- The email went to your spam folder.

If it has not arrived within 10 minutes, reply to this email and we will look into it.
