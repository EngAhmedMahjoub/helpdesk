# About Mahjoub Academy

### Who are we?

Mahjoub Academy sells online programming courses. Each course is bought once and kept for life. Students watch video lessons in their browser, download the source code, and receive a certificate when they complete a course.

### How do I reach support?

Write to support@helpdesk.mahjoub.io. Every answer comes back from that address, in the same email thread, so reply to it whenever you need us again.
